How the Ladder works

Everyone starts at Level 1. A bad week drops you down the ladder; once you've hit rock bottom, good weeks let you climb back up (capped at Level 2).

  • Level 1 — Survivor. Pick one team to win straight up. Can't reuse a team all season. Win: 1 pt, stay at Level 1. Tie: 0.5 pt, drop to Level 2. Loss: 0 pts, drop to Level 2.
  • Level 2 — 1 game ATS. Win: 1 pt, stay. Tie: 0.5 pt, drop to Level 3. Loss: 0 pts, drop to Level 3.
  • Level 3 — 2 games ATS. Win both, or win one + tie one: 1 pt, stay (or climb to Level 2 if you've ever hit Level 4). Tie both: 0.5 pt, drop to Level 4. Any loss: 0 pts, drop to Level 4.
  • Level 4 — 3 games ATS (parlay style). Any loss: 0 pts, stay. All ties: 0.5 pt, stay. At least one win with no losses: 1 pt, climb to Level 3.

Reaching Level 4 activates climbing for the rest of the season — from then on, a winning week at Level 2 or 3 moves you up a level (never higher than Level 2). A missed pick in a finalized week counts as a loss.
